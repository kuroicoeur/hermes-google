Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

This OAuth app is used by one person only: its owner. It lets the owner's personal Hermes AI assistant read and edit the owner's own Google Sheets and Google Drive files.

  • The only Google data accessed is the owner's own data.
  • Login tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer.
  • No data is sold, shared, or given to anyone else.
  • No data is collected from any other person.

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
